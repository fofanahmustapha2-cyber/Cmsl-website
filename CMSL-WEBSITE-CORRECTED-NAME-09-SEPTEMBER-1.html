OUR INSTITUTION

About the Council of Mosques

Who we are

The Council of Mosques - Sierra Leone brings mosques together through a national membership registry, institutional coordination, verified mosque profiles and development support.

Our purpose

CMSL works to strengthen mosque administration, improve facilities, support Islamic education and build cooperation among mosque communities throughout Sierra Leone.

INSTITUTIONAL LEADERSHIP

Leadership and administration

GOVERNANCE

Policies, rules and standards

Membership and registration

Mosque records must be submitted accurately. Each Registration PIN is assigned to one application, and each approved mosque receives one permanent CMSL Reference Code.

Information integrity

Mosque representatives are responsible for keeping institutional, leadership and facility information accurate and up to date.

Safeguarding and conduct

CMSL programs should respect Islamic values, community welfare, dignity, lawful conduct and responsible treatment of children and vulnerable persons.

Financial transparency

Published development projects should show their scope, total area, cost per square metre, progress and verified updates.

CMSL PROGRAMS
